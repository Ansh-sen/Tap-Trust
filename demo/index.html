THE FRICTIONLESS JOURNEY

Three simple beats.
Zero customer resistance.

Designed to feel effortless for customers of any tech ability.

STEP 01 PHYSICAL TAP
⌁

Customer taps or scans

At the checkout counter, table, mirror, or entrance, the customer taps their smartphone against the TAP TRUST stand or scans the high-contrast QR backup.

STEP 02 INSTANT BROWSER
⚡

The destination opens

The customer's native browser launches immediately. No app store installation, no sign-ups, no permissions, and no typing URLs on a keyboard.

STEP 03 HIGH VALUE ACTION
★

Customer acts in seconds

They leave a 5-star Google review, book their next appointment, copy guest Wi-Fi, or message your team on WhatsApp while they are still in your venue.
